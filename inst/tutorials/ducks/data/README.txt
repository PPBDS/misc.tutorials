ducks tutorial data: Delimited files (CSV)

mallards.csv
  GPS tracking data for 52 Mallard ducks (Anas platyrhynchos) fitted with
  solar-powered collars at Lake Constance, on the German/Swiss/Austrian
  border (~47.5N, 9.5E). One row per GPS fix; seven columns: timestamp,
  latitude, longitude, speed, heading, duck identifier, and species name.
  Source: Korner, Sauter, Fiedler & Jenni (2016), "Variable allocation of
  activity to daylight and night in the mallard," Animal Behaviour,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anbehav.2016.02.026 — archived on Movebank
  (https://movebank.org/). License: CC-BY.
  Teaching version: the original Movebank download had 748,082 rows mixing
  GPS fixes and accelerometer records across 36 columns; this file keeps only
  the GPS rows and the seven movement-analysis columns.
